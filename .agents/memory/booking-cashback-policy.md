---
name: Booking cashback policy
description: Settlement timing and single review-gated patient reward agreed with the user.
---
Paid appointments remain in provider escrow until the doctor or assigned active assistant marks the visit completed. The configured deduction is split between platform revenue and reserved patient cashback; the patient receives that reserved share only after an eligible review, not a second independent review bonus.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose completion-time settlement and a single review-gated reward. With the current fixed 50 EGP deduction and equal shares, 500 EGP means 450 provider, 25 platform, and 25 patient reserve. Existing welcome gifts remain separate from appointment proceeds.

**How to apply:** Preserve the configured review delay, transactional reserve release, and replay protection. Never automatically complete real appointments to repair reports. Display pending escrow separately from available balance.
